# Community Code of Conduct

Digital Ghana is committed to a respectful, safe and constructive community.

Contributors must communicate professionally, welcome good-faith participation, respect privacy and source communities, avoid harassment or discrimination, and focus disagreements on evidence and public benefit. Unacceptable behavior includes threats, harassment, discriminatory language, doxxing, deliberate misinformation, disclosure of private security reports and misuse of project access.

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations. A private conduct-reporting channel and named enforcement team must be published before broad community onboarding. Until then, report concerns privately to the repository owner.

This concise interim policy should be replaced or supplemented by an approved standard code of conduct before the first public contribution campaign.
